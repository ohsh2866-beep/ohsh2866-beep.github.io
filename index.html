Daily AI news, explained in three minutes

Trend Radar Daily is a working publishing service. Every day it gathers AI news from public sources, writes short explainers, produces short-form videos, and publishes them to its own channels in Korean and Arabic.

What this service does

Seven pieces of content are produced and published every day across two language brands.

Collect

Every day at noon KST

Headlines are gathered from Google News and Google Trends across Korean, English and Arabic sources, plus an evergreen library of AI terms and tools.

Write & produce

Article, cards, narrated video

Each topic becomes a long-form blog article, a card-news set, and a narrated vertical video with text-to-speech narration and licensed background music.

Publish

To our own channels only

Videos go to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, articles to the blog. We publish exclusively to accounts we own — this service never posts on behalf of third parties.
