Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

1. Overview

Trend Radar ("the Service") is an AI-assisted news briefing service operated by an individual operator. This website is informational only: it has no user accounts, no comment system, and no forms. We do not collect, store, or sell personal information from visitors.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No cookies are set by this website.
  • No analytics trackers are embedded by us.
  • No personal data is requested from, or stored about, visitors.

3. Platform APIs Used by the Service

The Service publishes content to the operator's own channels using official platform APIs. In doing so, the Service processes only the operator's own account credentials and publishing metadata — never third-party user data.

  • TikTok Content Posting API (scopes: video.upload, video.publish): used solely to upload and publish videos to the operator's own TikTok accounts. Data received from TikTok is limited to OAuth access tokens, the account open ID, and publishing status. Tokens are stored locally on the operator's machine, are never shared with third parties, and are deleted when no longer needed. See also the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube Data API: used to upload videos to the operator's own channels, subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram API: used to publish reels to the operator's own accounts, subject to the Meta Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Security

API credentials are stored in access-restricted local files on the operator's own equipment and are used exclusively for automated publishing to the operator's own accounts. No databases of personal information are maintained.

5. Children's Privacy

The Service does not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above reflects the latest revision.
